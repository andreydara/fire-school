# Trainer rubric — Galičica capstone

Use this as a quick discussion aid rather than a formal grading system.

## 1. Evidence

**Strong:** Claims clearly follow from the displayed map/graph/table.  
**Needs work:** Claims exceed what the evidence shows.

## 2. Terminology

Look for correct separation of:

- fire history / occurrence;
- burned area / severity;
- spectral vs ecological recovery;
- fire weather;
- vegetation/fuel-condition proxies;
- susceptibility;
- risk.

## 3. Uncertainty

**Strong:** Names the limitation that most affects interpretation.  
**Needs work:** Generic “more data are needed” without explaining why.

## 4. Management relevance

**Strong:** Suggests a realistic monitoring, field-validation or prioritization action.  
**Needs work:** Makes operational recommendations unsupported by the analysis.

## 5. Communication

**Strong:** One clear map, concise findings, no unnecessary technical detail.  
**Needs work:** Replays the notebook rather than answering the team's question.

## Suggested trainer synthesis

After Team E, connect the five presentations into one chain:

**history → disturbance → recovery → conditions → susceptibility → management**

Then ask the class which links are:

- directly observed;
- inferred from proxies;
- dependent on model assumptions;
- still missing.
