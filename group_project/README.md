# Galičica group project

The capstone reuses outputs from the hands-on sessions rather than introducing a separate large analysis.

Planned teams:

- Fire history
- Burned area and severity
- Post-fire recovery
- Fire weather and fuel conditions
- Susceptibility and management priorities

Each group should produce:

- 1 main map
- 1 graph or table where appropriate
- 3 defensible findings
- 1 important limitation
- 1 management implication
